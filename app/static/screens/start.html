How it works

Describe the place, and shoppers walk it before you open.

Build the space

Say what the business is, and the agent picks a space that fits, builds it in 3D, moves fixtures as needed, and lets shoppers walk it.

Connect records

Upload receipts and display schedules, and the decision model calibrates to the shoppers of that space. No records yet? Start with a sample.

Plans to test · Run

Every plan you want to try runs at once, on the same shoppers and the same visits. The plan is the only thing that differs, so the difference is the effect.

Final layout · Report

The best plan is placed in the space, with how much it improves and why, on a single page.

Calibrated to People, not to Answers. Not the probability that an answer sounds right, but the probability that the shoppers in that space actually do it. SimOff-1 is validated on 6 weeks of real purchases the model never saw.
